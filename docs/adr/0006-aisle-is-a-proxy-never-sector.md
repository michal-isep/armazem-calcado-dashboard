# Aisle is a labelled proxy and is never called Sector

Every product has Sector `PF`, so sector share is always 100%. We offer aisle (first letter of the pick location) as an additional view, tagged as a proxy and named separately (aisle share of picks). Calling it Sector would make a different concept look like the required one. Rare aisles (C, E, Z, G) stay in their own bucket because their meaning is unknown.
