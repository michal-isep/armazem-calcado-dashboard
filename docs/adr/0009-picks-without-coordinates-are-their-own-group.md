# Picks at locations without coordinates are reported as their own group

`Storage_Location` places 191,583 of 215,192 picks (89.0%). The other 23,609 are at codes with no coordinates in any file, and 22,979 of them (10.7% of all picks) are at one code, `RC-01`. It is named like the navigation points (`LC-`, `CC-`, `RC-`), not like a shelf (`H-06-13`), and the navigation file stops at `RC-08`, so `RC-01` to `RC-07` are missing. Because the aisle is the first letter, aisle R looks like the second-busiest aisle (25,925 picks, 12.0%), yet only about 1.4% of all picks are on real R shelves. We do not drop these picks, guess where they are, or assign them to a shelf. Position figures use only picks with coordinates and say how many were left out. The aisle definition of ADR 0006 does not change, so A1 and X3 still add up to all picks; the Warehouse zones page adds a coverage table and a note that most of aisle R is `RC-01`. What `RC-01` is (a dock, a staging point, a replenishment point) is unknown and is a question for the teacher or the data owner.
